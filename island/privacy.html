Island Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11 · Effective date 2026-11-18

Island is built to keep your information on your Mac. This policy explains the little that does leave it. It is provided by MundaneApps (“we”, “us”).

1. What stays on your Mac

Audio, transcripts, captions, meeting summaries, answers to your questions, calendar and reminder data, music information and notes are processed on your Mac (using Apple’s on-device models) and are never sent to us. If you turn on “Remember voices”, Island keeps a short numeric voice print (not audio) for each person you name, on your Mac only; “Forget all voices” deletes them.

2. What Island sends, and why

  • Updates: once a day Island downloads a small file from mundaneapps.com to see if there’s a new version. Like any web request, this includes your IP address.
  • Weather and lyrics: if you turn these on, Island asks a weather service for your approximate location’s forecast and a lyrics service for the song playing.
  • Island Pro: to confirm a Mac, Island sends your licence, a public key created on that Mac (in its Secure Enclave where available), and the 6-digit code. From time to time it sends the licence ID and that public key to renew. No hardware serial numbers or other identifiers are collected.
  • Crash reports (only if you agree): after Island’s first crash it asks once whether to send reports; it’s off until you say yes, and you can change it in Settings → General. A report contains Island’s version, the macOS version, the Mac model and Apple’s technical crash data (where in Island’s code it happened). No names, notes, audio or files. Reports are deleted after 180 days.
  • Sharing you choose: if you send a recap to Notion, Island sends it straight to Notion with the integration token you entered (stored only on your Mac). Email, Slack and Word exports go through the apps you pick; nothing passes through us.

3. What we store

  • For buyers: name and email (from Paddle), the purchase reference, the licence, the public keys and renewal dates of confirmed Macs, and records of emails we sent you.
  • Codes are stored only as one-way hashes and expire after 10 minutes. Rate-limit records use hashed IP addresses and are deleted after a day.
  • Payment details are handled by Paddle, our Merchant of Record, under Paddle’s privacy policy. We never see your card.

4. Service providers

Paddle (payments), Supabase (licence database and functions, hosted in the EU), and Resend (email). They process data only to provide these services.

5. How long

We keep purchase and licence records for as long as the licence exists and as required for tax and accounting. Ask us to delete your data at info@mundaneapps.com; deleting it cancels the licence.

6. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. We don’t sell data, show ads, or use analytics in Island. Our website may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free visit counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics).
