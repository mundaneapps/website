Space Voyage

How far can you fly?

Launch from Earth, dodge asteroids, and keep your fuel topped up. Collect crystals, discover 23 regions, and build a fleet for your next attempt.

Play as a guest, or choose Google sign-in for online progress and leaderboards. One-thumb controls keep each voyage close at hand.

Android · Intended for ages 13+ · Contains ads and optional in-app purchases

Preparing for closed testing. The game is not yet publicly available. Tester enrollment and release availability will be shared when the test is live.

Ask about testing

Before you launch

The game includes optional rewarded ads for a revive and interstitial ads between some runs. Ad availability varies. Crystal packs and other available purchases are optional, and payments are handled by Google Play. Guest play does not require an account, but advertising services can still process device and ad data.

Space Voyage is intended for players aged 13 and older. This audience statement is not an official store content rating. Testing can involve bugs, balance changes, and changes to saved progress; keep that in mind before making purchases in a test build.
