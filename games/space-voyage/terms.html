Space Voyage game terms

MundaneApps · Updated 20 October 2026

Who the game is for

Space Voyage is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you are under the age of legal adulthood where you live, ask a parent or guardian to review these terms and the privacy policy with you. Follow local age, consent, and purchase requirements. This age guidance is not a substitute for the store's content rating.

Accounts and fair play

Guest play does not require an account. Online progress, leaderboard submissions, and account-linked features require sign-in and connectivity. Keep your sign-in secure and choose a public name that does not expose private information. Do not cheat, tamper with purchases or scores, impersonate others, or try to access another player's data. Invalid runs or abusive public names may be removed from public rankings.

Ads and optional purchases

The game contains advertising and optional in-app purchases. Any item offered, its price, and the payment terms are shown through the game and Google Play before purchase. Virtual crystals and game items are for use within Space Voyage; they are not cash, cannot be redeemed for money, and are not a real-world investment. Google Play handles payments and refund requests, subject to its terms and applicable consumer rights. Deleting your account does not automatically refund purchases.

Testing and availability

Closed-test builds are unfinished and may have bugs, balance changes, downtime, or changes to saved progress. Test access depends on enrollment and release availability. We do not promise uninterrupted online services, ad availability, or that a test score or balance will carry into a later version.

Your data and leaving the game

The privacy policy explains account data, public profiles, purchases, analytics, and ads. You can request deletion in the game or through our account-deletion page.

Contact and your rights

For support or questions, email mundaneapps@gmail.com. Nothing on this page limits rights you have under applicable consumer or privacy law. Google Play and third-party services have their own terms.
