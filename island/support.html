Island support

Answers to common questions about installing Island, the Pro trial and licences. Still stuck? Email info@mundaneapps.com; a real person reads every message, usually within a couple of days.

Installing

  • Requirements: macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. A notch isn’t required; without one, the island sits at the top centre of the screen.
  • “Island can’t be opened” or “Apple could not verify”: Island is signed by MundaneApps rather than through the Mac App Store. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once; updates install without asking.
  • Permissions: setup asks for each one (microphone, calendar, Bluetooth and so on) and says why. You can change them later in Island → Settings → General → Run setup again.
  • Updates: Island checks once a day and installs only updates signed by MundaneApps. You can also check in Settings → General.

Pro trial and buying

  • Every Pro feature is free for 14 days from the first time you open Island, with no account or card. After that Island keeps working with its free features; your notes and settings stay.
  • Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase for every Mac you personally use. See the buy page for the current price.
  • Payments are handled by our reseller Paddle. For receipts, invoices or payment questions, visit paddle.net.
  • Refunds within 14 days, no questions asked: see the refund policy.

Your licence

  • Turning on Pro: double-click the Island Pro.mundanelicense file from your purchase email (or use Island → Settings → Island Pro → Open licence file…). Island emails a 6-digit code to the purchase address to confirm the Mac.
  • Another Mac: do the same on that Mac; each Mac gets its own code. There’s no limit on your own Macs.
  • Lost the email: use “Lost your licence?” in Island → Settings → Island Pro, or on the buy page. It goes to the email you bought with.
  • “This Mac needs to be confirmed again”: usually after moving to a new Mac or resetting it. Click the button and enter the emailed code.
  • Pro paused after a week offline: each Mac renews itself about once a week when it’s online. Connect to the internet and it renews by itself; free features always work offline.
  • Changed your email address: write to us from the old or new address with your Paddle receipt and we’ll move the licence.

Feedback and ideas

We’d love to hear what works, what doesn’t and what you wish Island did. In Island, open Settings → General → Send feedback (or the menu bar icon → Send Feedback…); it starts an email with your Island and macOS versions filled in. Or write to info@mundaneapps.com.

Your data

Meeting audio, transcripts and summaries are processed on your Mac and never sent to us. See the Island privacy policy, or delete your Island data.
