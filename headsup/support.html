HeadsUp support

Have a question, ran into a bug, or want to request a feature? Email us at info@mundaneapps.com, a real person reads every message, usually within a couple of days.

Report a problem

For help with HeadsUp, include your device model, app version, and a description of what happened. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Feature requests

Tell us the exact cadence, notification behavior, or workflow you wish HeadsUp handled differently. Concrete examples are especially useful. The in-app feature-request form (Settings → Feedback) sends straight to the same inbox.

Account and data questions

For anything about your account, your data, or deleting it, see our HeadsUp privacy policy and the HeadsUp account-deletion page, or just email us directly.

Subscriptions & billing

Billing stays with the store where you subscribed. For cancellation links and what happens when an account is deleted, use our account-deletion guide.
