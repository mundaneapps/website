Install in a minute.

Needs macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. Island comes from us, not the App Store, and this early version isn’t notarised by Apple yet. So the first time you open it, macOS warns that it “could not verify” Island. That means Apple hasn’t scanned this app, not that anything was found. You’ll allow it once, and never see the warning again.

  1. DownloadOpen the Island .dmg and drag Island to Applications.
  2. Open itDouble-click Island. When macOS says it could not verify it, click Done (not Move to Bin).
  3. Allow it, onceOpen System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Island was blocked”, click Open Anyway, and confirm with Touch ID or your password.
  4. That’s itUpdates install from inside Island, are checked to be signed by MundaneApps, and never show the warning again.

Why it’s safe to allow: Island runs entirely on your Mac and never uploads your audio, notes or files. Each release is signed by MundaneApps, refunds are open for 14 days, and a real person answers at info@mundaneapps.com.

Questions

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

Island keeps working with all the free features. Pro features lock, and your notes, recordings’ notes and settings stay exactly where they are. Buy Pro any time to unlock them again.

How many Macs can I use Pro on?

All the Macs you personally use. The first time you use your licence on a Mac, Island emails a 6-digit code to your purchase address to confirm it. After that it renews quietly in the background.

When does the 14-day trial start?

The first time you use a Pro feature (taking meeting notes, asking a question, live captions and so on), or when you press “Start 14-day free trial” in Settings. Installing Island and using the free features doesn’t start it.

Why does Island check in now and then?

Each Mac holds a confirmation that renews itself automatically in the background when you’re online, with no codes or emails. If a Mac stays offline long enough for it to run out, Pro pauses until it can renew. Free features always work offline.

Does Island need a MacBook with a notch?

No. On Macs and displays without a notch, the island sits at the top centre of the screen and works the same way.

Is my meeting audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Speech recognition, summaries and answers run on your Mac. Island only talks to MundaneApps to confirm your licence and check for updates.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of purchase. See the refund policy. A refund turns Pro off on your Macs; your notes stay.

I lost my licence email.

Use “Lost your licence?” in Island → Settings → Island Pro, or on the buy page. We’ll send it to the email you bought with.
