Delete your Island data

Island has no account. You don’t sign up to use Island or its trial, so there is no account to delete. Here is how to remove what exists.

On your Mac

Everything Island makes stays on your Mac. To remove it:

  1. Quit Island (menu bar icon → Quit Island) and move Island from Applications to the Bin.
  2. Delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Island (in Finder: Go → Go to Folder…). It holds the trial dates, your licence and this Mac’s confirmation.
  3. Delete Island’s settings: run defaults delete com.mundaneapps.island in Terminal.
  4. Meeting notes are ordinary Markdown files in the notes folder you chose in Settings → Meetings & notes. Delete them in Finder if you don’t want them; Island never deletes your notes.

If you bought Island Pro

We keep your name and email, the purchase reference, your licence, and the confirmations of the Macs you use (a public key and dates; no hardware identifiers). To have these deleted, email info@mundaneapps.com from the address you bought with and ask us to delete your Island data. We’ll confirm within 30 days.

  • Deleting your data cancels the licence: Pro turns off on your Macs at their next weekly check-in, and the licence can’t be recovered.
  • We may keep the payment record that tax and accounting law requires. Payment details themselves are held by Paddle, our reseller; see Paddle’s privacy policy for their part.
  • If you also want your money back, ask for a refund first (within 14 days): see the refund policy.
