Island Refund Policy

Version 2026-11 · Effective date 2026-11-18

We want you to be happy with Island Pro. Try every Pro feature free for 14 days before you buy, and if Pro still isn’t right for you, you can get your money back.

14-day refunds

  • Ask within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, no reason needed.
  • Request it at paddle.net (find your order with the email you bought with), reply to your Paddle receipt, or write to info@mundaneapps.com.
  • Refunds are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record, to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • After 14 days we still consider refunds where Island Pro doesn’t work as described or where consumer law requires it.

What a refund means

A refund cancels the licence: Island Pro turns off on your Macs at their next weekly renewal. Island and its free features keep working, and your notes and settings are untouched.

Launch price

Launch-price licences are limited in number. A refunded launch-price licence is not returned to the pool.
